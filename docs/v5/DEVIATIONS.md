# Deviations from the pre-registration

Recorded as they happen. The pre-registration (`PREREGISTRATION.md`, commit 0c706e8) is unchanged.

1. **Tuning budget for the embedding models (19 Nov 2026, before any evaluation seed).**
   The pre-registration says 25 TPE trials per method. One embedding-model trial took 3 to 4.5 minutes on the shared 5 GB GPU, so 25 trials for six embedding configurations would have taken more than seven hours. The embedding models were tuned with **12 trials each**, and their search space was narrowed (batch size 512 or 1024; 30 or 60 epochs; 256 or 1024 shared negatives for the softmax loss). The GNN variants kept 25 trials, with the wider space in `scripts/46`. NBFNet used a four-point grid (dimension 32 or 64, 2 or 3 layers; 240 s each). The unequal trial counts favour the GNNs; the wall-clock time spent on each method is in the `trials` list of each `configs/model_v5/substitution_*.json`.
2. **Discarded first attempts.** Two earlier embedding-model tuning runs were stopped after a few trials for the reason above; their logs are kept as `logs/v5_tune_kge_first_attempt.log` and `logs/v5_tune_kge_second_attempt.log`. Their trials were not used.
3. **Tuning scenario.** Scenario seed 100 hides a random 10% of links of the same network that the evaluation seeds use. No hidden link of an evaluation seed is trained on, but the tuned settings reflect this network; this is stated in the paper as a limitation.
4. **NBFNet is our implementation** of the published idea (`src/suschain/models/nbfnet.py`), simplified to run on a 5 GB GPU; it is not the authors' code, and it is trained for a fixed wall-clock budget (600 s per seed).
5. **Not run: Neural Common Neighbor and BUDDY.** They score a pair from the neighbourhoods of both nodes. Co-supply, common neighbours and Adamic-Adar are in the study, and the question the models would answer (can message passing count common neighbours?) is addressed by NBFNet's path counting. Stated as a limitation.
6. **H5 computed in a separate script (20 Nov 2026, after the confirmatory run).** `scripts/48` reports the risk budget averaged over seeds, while H5 is stated per seed. `scripts/54_v5_h5_penalty.py` computes it with the functions and random streams of `scripts/48` (`customer_matrix`, `budget_study`, seed `1000 + s`). The calibration share is half of the customers, the setting of the manuscript; the other sizes are reported alongside. No other analysis choice was made after seeing the results.
7. **The learned penalty at lambda = 5.** The confirmatory run trained the best GNN with lambda = 1 (H4) and lambda = 5. Only lambda = 1 is named in the pre-registration; every result at lambda = 5 is reported as exploratory.
8. **Reproducibility of the tuning scenario.** Rerunning seed 100 with the frozen settings (`v5_confirmatory_tuning.json`) reproduces the embedding models' tuning scores exactly and the GNN and NBFNet scores within 0.0035 of hit@10 (non-deterministic GPU kernels; NBFNet trains for a fixed wall-clock time). The risk levels alpha are taken from this rerun, as planned.
9. **Reverse-link exposure (found 20 Nov 2026, after the confirmatory run).** The substitution scenario hides directed links one at a time, so for about 12% of hidden links the opposite link stays visible. In about 14% of queries a correct answer has a visible link from the customer, which learned rankers can use and co-supply cannot. `scripts/55_v5_reverse_exposure.py` repeats the comparisons on the remaining queries from the stored per-query outcomes (no retraining): H1-H3 hold (all 10/10 seeds, p = 0.002), margins shrink, and the exploratory NBFNet-over-RotatE lead disappears. Reported in the paper as exploratory.
10. **Penalty-size diagnostic (20 Nov 2026).** `scripts/56_v5_penalty_scale.py` measures the penalty relative to the base loss in the first 60 training steps: 1-2% under the softmax loss against 12-17% under binary cross-entropy at lambda = 1. Exploratory; it explains the inert H4 result.
11. **Embedding-model search space** was narrowed after the first of the two discarded attempts (see item 2).
12. **Trase:** T3 and the shift curves use model seed 0 only; NBFNet was evaluated with a 300 s budget against a 90 s tuning grid. Not listed in `PREREGISTRATION_TRASE.md`.
13. **Decision-time weights (20 Nov 2026, after the confirmatory run).** The risk of a shortlist needs no labels, so the shift weight can also be chosen per shortlist or per customer when the list is made. `scripts/57_v5_control_modes.py` compares these rules with the two calibrated rules on the stored per-query curves (no retraining). Exploratory. For RotatE and a 20% target: weight per customer 13.8 points of hit@10 lost, guaranteed rule 22.1 points with 25 calibration customers, 10.1 with 100 and 5.2 with half of the customers.
14. **Trase importer-level check (20 Nov 2026).** For a learned ranker all queries of one importer share candidates, answers and scores, so the per-query mean weights importers by the number of exporters they dropped. With importers weighted equally (model seed 0): RotatE 62.5% against co-supply 56.0%, ahead in 6 of 7 years (p = 0.031); NBFNet level with co-supply. Also in `scripts/57`. Exploratory.
15. **H5 tolerance.** `PREREGISTRATION.md` states H5 with a tolerance of 0.001 and no fixed calibration share; the manuscript uses half of the customers (item 6) and H5 passes without the tolerance.
